Frequently Asked Questions

All-on-4 is a full arch approach in which a complete set of replacement teeth is attached to four implants placed in the jaw. Suitability depends on your jaw and overall health.

For suitable candidates, a temporary or final set of teeth can be attached on the same day the implants are placed. Whether this is appropriate is decided at your evaluation.

Zygomatic implants are longer implants anchored in the cheekbone. They may be considered when there is not enough upper jaw bone for conventional implants.

They snap or screw onto implants, which holds them more firmly in place than suction or adhesive alone.

Most adults in reasonable health can be evaluated. Bone, gum health and medical history are reviewed before a recommendation is made.
